About Me

I am a highly motivated Postdoctoral researcher with a deep passion for developing innovative methods for water and glacier monitoring, using cutting-edge technologies such as Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) and Light Detection and Ranging (LIDAR) imagery. With a strong foundation in remote sensing and data analysis, I am constantly seeking to push the boundaries of what can be achieved in these fields.

My work revolves around solving complex challenges related to environmental monitoring, specifically in the context of water bodies and glaciers. I am committed to leveraging cloud-based solutions like Google Earth Engine (GEE) and GEDI data to improve monitoring processes and gain deeper insights into the dynamic changes happening in our environment.

Research Interests

  • Water and Glacier Monitoring: Developing methods for large-scale monitoring of inland water levels, glacier morphology, and environmental changes using SAR and LIDAR data.
  • Vegetation Monitoring: Developing methods to understand the phenological cycles of vegetation using Hyperspectral and multispectral imagery.
  • Geospatial Data Analysis: Advanced techniques in data processing and analysis to interpret large volumes of geospatial data for more efficient environmental monitoring.
